TripBanBan Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

TripBanBan (旅行伴伴) is provided by TripBanBan. This Privacy Policy explains how the Android application handles information.

1. Local-first travel data

TripBanBan is designed as a local-first application. Travel projects, traveler names, expenses, shared-fund transactions, settlement information, exchange-rate records, settings, and photo attachments are stored on the user's device.

TripBanBan does not operate an account server for this ledger data and does not automatically upload trip data to TripBanBan servers.

2. Backup, import, export, and sharing

TripBanBan Plus can export a .tripbanban backup that may contain ledger data and local photo attachments. Export happens only when the user explicitly requests it. After export, the user controls where the file is saved or shared, and the privacy practices of that destination apply.

Import reads a .tripbanban file selected by the user and can replace local app data only after user confirmation. Google Drive backup and restore are not part of the production product.

3. Photos and files

If the user chooses to attach a photo or import/export a file, TripBanBan accesses only the media or file selected through the Android system picker or an equivalent user-initiated flow. These files are used for the requested app feature.

4. Google Play purchases

TripBanBan Plus is offered as a Google Play one-time purchase. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play. TripBanBan receives purchase and entitlement status needed to unlock Plus features; payment-card details are not processed or stored by TripBanBan. Google's own privacy terms apply to Google Play transactions.

5. Network access

The app may use network access for Google Play purchase services and, if a remote exchange-rate source is enabled, to retrieve exchange-rate data. Network service providers may receive standard connection information such as an IP address as part of providing those services.

6. Advertising and tracking

TripBanBan does not include advertising SDKs and is not designed to perform cross-app advertising tracking.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on the device until the user deletes trips or data, clears app storage, uninstalls the app, or replaces data through an import operation. Exported backup files are controlled by the user and must be deleted separately from the storage location selected by the user.

8. Children

TripBanBan is a general travel utility and does not provide an online account service designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when application functionality, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.

10. Contact
