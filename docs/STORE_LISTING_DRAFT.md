## Full description — zh-TW

旅行伴伴 TripBanBan 是一款以多人旅行分帳為核心的旅遊記帳工具。

建立旅程後即可加入旅伴、記錄每筆支出、管理共同基金，並在旅程結束時快速算出誰應該付給誰，減少人工對帳的麻煩。

主要功能：

• 多旅程專案管理與快速切換
• 依旅伴、類別、日期查看支出紀錄與統計
• 支援多人分攤與每人付款／應分攤／淨額統計
• 共同基金繳入、補充、退款與每人淨匯入明細
• 自動產生最後轉帳建議
• 支援多種常用貨幣與手動／快取匯率
• 10 種常用介面語言
• 本機優先，旅行帳本保存在自己的裝置

TripBanBan Plus 為一次買斷，可解鎖超過 3 位旅伴，以及完整 `.tripbanban` 備份匯入／匯出功能。

不需要建立 TripBanBan 雲端帳號，也不包含廣告。

## Short description — en-US

Split travel expenses, manage shared funds, and settle group trips with ease.

## Full description — en-US

TripBanBan is a local-first travel expense and group settlement app designed for trips with friends and family.

Create a trip, add travelers, record expenses, manage a shared fund, and let TripBanBan calculate who should pay whom at the end of the journey.

Key features:

• Manage multiple trip projects and switch between them quickly
• Review expenses by traveler, category, date, and payment source
• Track paid, allocated, and net amounts for every traveler
• Manage shared-fund deposits, top-ups, refunds, and per-person net contributions
• Generate final transfer suggestions automatically
• Support common currencies with manual/cached exchange rates
• 10 commonly used interface languages
• Local-first ledger storage on your own device

TripBanBan Plus is a one-time purchase that unlocks more than three travelers per trip and complete `.tripbanban` backup import/export.

No TripBanBan cloud account is required. The app contains no advertising.
