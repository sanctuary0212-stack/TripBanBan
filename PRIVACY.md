# TripBanBan Privacy Policy / 旅行伴伴隱私權政策

**Effective date / 生效日期: October 4, 2026 / 2026 年 10 月 4 日**

TripBanBan (旅行伴伴) is a local-first travel expense and settlement app. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and when it may leave the device.

旅行伴伴（TripBanBan）是一款以本機資料為主的旅遊記帳、分帳與結算 App。本政策說明 App 會處理哪些資訊、資料儲存位置，以及哪些情況下資料可能離開裝置。

## 1. Information handled / 處理的資料

Depending on the features you use, TripBanBan may handle:
- trip names and trip settings;
- traveler/member names;
- expense records, split amounts, shared-fund transactions, settlements, currencies and exchange-rate snapshots;
- photos or files that you explicitly select as attachments;
- app preferences and local backup/import information.

依照您使用的功能，TripBanBan 可能處理：
- 旅程名稱與旅程設定；
- 旅伴／成員名稱；
- 支出、分攤、公基金、結算、幣別與匯率快照；
- 您主動選取作為附件的照片或檔案；
- App 偏好設定及本機備份／匯入相關資訊。

## 2. Local-first storage / 本機優先儲存

Core travel and expense data is stored locally on the user's device using the app's local database. TripBanBan does not operate an account server that automatically uploads this core ledger data.

核心旅遊與帳務資料儲存在使用者裝置的本機資料庫。TripBanBan 目前沒有自建帳號伺服器，也不會自動將核心帳務資料上傳至 TripBanBan 伺服器。

## 3. Photos and files / 照片與檔案

When you choose to attach a photo, import a backup, or export/share a file, TripBanBan accesses only the media or file selected through the Android system picker or another user-initiated flow. These items are used only for the feature you requested.

當您主動附加照片、匯入備份，或匯出／分享檔案時，TripBanBan 僅存取您透過 Android 系統選擇器或其他使用者主動操作所選取的內容，並僅用於您要求的功能。

## 4. Backup, export and sharing / 備份、匯出與分享

TripBanBan may create a local `.tripbanban` backup, CSV file, settlement image, or other locally generated export when the user requests it. After export or sharing, the user controls the destination, and the privacy practices of that destination or receiving app apply.

TripBanBan 可在使用者主動要求時建立本機 `.tripbanban` 備份、CSV、結算圖片或其他本機產生的匯出內容。匯出或分享後，資料由使用者選擇的儲存位置或接收 App 管理，並適用該服務的隱私權政策。

The current production build does not use Google Drive backup or restore.

目前正式版不使用 Google Drive 備份或還原。

## 5. Network access and exchange rates / 網路與匯率

The app can operate its core expense-splitting functions offline. If a remote exchange-rate source is enabled, the app may connect to that source to retrieve exchange-rate data. Network providers may receive standard connection information such as an IP address as part of delivering the request.

App 的核心記帳與分帳功能可離線使用。若啟用遠端匯率來源，App 可能連線至該來源以取得匯率資料；在提供網路服務的過程中，服務供應商可能接收到 IP 位址等標準連線資訊。

## 6. Advertising, analytics and tracking / 廣告、分析與追蹤

TripBanBan does not include advertising SDKs and is not designed to perform cross-app advertising tracking. The current production build does not include a third-party analytics SDK.

TripBanBan 不包含廣告 SDK，也不進行跨 App 廣告追蹤。目前正式版亦未包含第三方分析 SDK。

## 7. Data retention and deletion / 資料保存與刪除

Local data remains on the device until the user deletes relevant data, clears app storage, uninstalls the app, or replaces data through an import operation. Exported files must be deleted separately from the storage location selected by the user.

本機資料會保留在裝置上，直到使用者刪除相關資料、清除 App 儲存空間、解除安裝 App，或透過匯入操作覆蓋資料。已匯出的檔案需由使用者在所選儲存位置另外刪除。

## 8. Children / 兒童

TripBanBan is a general travel utility and is not designed as an online service directed specifically to children.

TripBanBan 是一般旅遊工具，並非專門針對兒童設計的線上服務。

## 9. Security / 資料安全

TripBanBan uses platform-provided storage and file-selection mechanisms where applicable. No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free, so users should protect their devices and exported backup files appropriately.

TripBanBan 在適用情況下使用平台提供的儲存與檔案選擇機制。任何電子儲存方式都無法保證完全無風險，因此建議使用者妥善保護裝置及匯出的備份檔案。

## 10. Changes to this policy / 政策變更

This policy may be updated when app functionality, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

若 App 功能、服務供應商或法規要求有所變更，本政策可能同步更新；重大變更發布時會更新上方生效日期。
